## Bug Fixes

### safe editing, removing and saving of meal plans

**Commit**: [`8c4cc03`](../../commit/8c4cc037be801c1c0e0ca1323757665f608a094f)  
**Author**: Austin Santos  

working-session persistence) with unit tests
- Edit a meal's servings or swap its recipe in one step; add
  "Remove from <day>" (previously impossible)
